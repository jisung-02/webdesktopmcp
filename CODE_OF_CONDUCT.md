# Code of Conduct

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

- Treat participants respectfully regardless of identity, background, or experience.
- Keep feedback specific to the work. Explain disagreements and accept corrections.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or send unwanted sexual content.
- Do not spam discussions or repeatedly disrupt collaboration.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation based on the severity and repetition of the behavior. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so would not expose private information or increase harm.

Use GitHub's **Report content** option for abusive content; see [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). For an ordinary moderation request that can be discussed safely in public, open an issue without personal or sensitive details. Do not use vulnerability reports for general conduct complaints.
